Toggle

Toggle buttons switch between a selected and an unselected state, for example to bookmark an item or turn on a setting. Following Material 3 Expressive, a selected toggle changes color and morphs its shape: round toggles become square and square toggles become round. Toggles come in filled, tonal, outlined and elevated variants and five sizes, and can be combined in a button group.
