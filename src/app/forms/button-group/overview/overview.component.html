Button Group

Button groups organize related buttons and toggles. In a standard group buttons are separated by a gap and widen slightly while pressed; in a connected group they are joined together, and the selected one becomes fully rounded, which makes connected groups a good fit for single or multi-select options. Toggles with a value are selected through the group's two-way bound value.
