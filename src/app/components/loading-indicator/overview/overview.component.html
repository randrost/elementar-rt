Loading Indicator

The Material 3 Expressive loading indicator shows indeterminate progress for short waits (under five seconds) by continuously morphing between a sequence of rounded shapes while it rotates. Use it in place of a circular progress spinner when a process has no measurable progress, for example on pull-to-refresh or while a small section of a page loads.
