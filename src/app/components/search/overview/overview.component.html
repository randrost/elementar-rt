Search

Search lets people enter a keyword or phrase to get relevant information. A search bar expands into a search view that shows suggestions or results while typing. Use the docked variant on medium and large windows, where the view opens as a panel below the bar, and the fullscreen variant on compact windows, where the view covers the whole screen. The input is an accessible combobox: arrow keys move through the results and Enter selects one.
