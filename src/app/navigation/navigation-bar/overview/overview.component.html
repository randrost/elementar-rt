Navigation Bar

Navigation bars let people switch between three to five primary destinations in an app. They sit at the bottom of compact windows, show an icon with a label for each destination, and highlight the active destination with a pill-shaped indicator. On medium windows use the wide layout, which places the label next to the icon.
